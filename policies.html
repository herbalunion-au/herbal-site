Policies

1. Practitioner‑Only Supply / 仅限执业供给

Herbal formulas are supplied under practitioner supervision to assessed patients only. No over‑the‑counter retail.

中药配方仅在执业医师监督下提供给已评估患者，非公开零售。

2. Prescriptions & Eligibility / 处方与适用对象

  • New clients with valid prescriptions may submit verification (image/link) for conversion to granules.
  • 新客户若持有效处方，可提交图片或链接以核对并换算为颗粒剂。
  • New clients without prescriptions require consultation and clinical assessment.
  • 无处方新客户需先进行问诊与临床评估。

3. Dispensing & Turnaround / 调配与时效

Allow 1–3 business days for dispensing after approval. Complex formulas may require additional time.

审核通过后，调配时间一般为1–3个工作日；复杂处方可能需更久。

4. Payment / 付款

Invoices will be issued after clinical approval. Accepted methods: bank transfer and major cards (if applicable). Prices are visible to logged‑in patients only.

临床审核通过后开具发票。可接受：转账及主要银行卡（如适用）。价格仅对已登录患者可见。

5. Shipping / 邮寄

Tracked shipping within Australia. Heat‑sensitive items may be limited during summer. Provide a safe delivery address.

澳洲境内跟踪邮寄；夏季高温可能限制部分品类。请提供安全收货地址。

6. Returns / 退换

For safety, custom prescriptions are not returnable once dispensed, except for errors on our part. Report transit damage within 48 hours with photos.

为保障安全，定制处方一经调配不予退换；若因我方错误将予以更正或退款。运输损坏请在48小时内提供照片反馈。

7. Privacy & Consent / 隐私与同意

We collect personal/health information solely for clinical care and order fulfilment. Data is transmitted securely; please avoid sending sensitive data over unsecured channels.

我们仅为临床照护与订单履行采集必要的个人/健康信息。数据采用加密传输；请避免通过不安全渠道提交敏感信息。

8. Adverse Events & Complaints / 不良反应与投诉

Cease use and contact us promptly if adverse reactions occur. For complaints, contact the clinic; if unresolved, you may escalate to relevant authorities.

如出现不良反应，请立即停用并联系本诊所。若投诉未获妥善解决，可向相关监管机构进一步反映。
